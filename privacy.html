Privacy Policy

Last updated · 26 September 2026

This policy describes how SEAI uses information when you use our website building service. We collect only what building your website requires — and nothing else.

1. What we collect

When you submit an intake form, we collect the information you provide: business name, type, description, location, contact details, logo, photos, style preferences, and goals. This information is used solely to build your website.

We also collect billing information needed to process your payment.

2. How it is used

  • To design, write, build, and deploy your website.
  • To communicate with you about your project and revisions.
  • To improve our service (aggregated, anonymized usage data only).

3. Access and control

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

4. Storage and security

Data is handled with least-privilege access, encrypted connections, and strictly scoped permissions. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share your data with third parties except where required to provide the service (payment processing, domain registration, hosting).

5. This website

The public website at seai.store does not use third-party analytics, trackers, or advertising cookies. We respect your privacy.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this policy as the service evolves. Questions: hello@seai.store.
