Privacy Policy

Last updated · 26 September 2026

This policy describes how SEAI handles information when you use our website and services — branding, advertising, digital marketing, websites, lead generation and growth. We collect what the work requires, and nothing more.

1. What we collect

When you contact us or complete onboarding, we collect: business and contact details (name, business name, email, phone, industry, website, address), goals and budgets you share, brand assets you upload (logos, photos, videos, guidelines), and platform access you grant (via invites — never passwords).

Advertising platforms (Meta, Google) process campaign data under your ad accounts and their own terms; SEAI accesses them with the permissions you grant and uses them only to deliver the agreed services.

We also collect billing information needed to process your invoices and payments.

2. How it is used

  • To scope, perform and report on the services you buy — strategy, campaigns, content, websites, lead funnels, follow-ups.
  • To communicate with you — proposals, approvals, reports, invoices, support.
  • To improve our delivery (aggregated, anonymized analysis only).
  • To meet legal and tax obligations (invoicing, GST records, statutory retention).

3. Client portal data

Your client dashboard stores project data, approvals and invoices. Access is scoped to your account. On request, we will export or delete your data subject to legal retention requirements (tax invoices must be retained by law).

4. Access and control

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. We respond within 30 days.

5. Storage and security

Data is handled with least-privilege access, encrypted connections and scoped permissions. We do not sell personal data. We share data with third parties only where required to provide the service (hosting, domain, payment processing via Razorpay, advertising platforms under your accounts).

6. This website

The public website at seai.store does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. Demonstration builds may include placeholder analytics in example configurations only.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this policy as the service evolves. Questions: workwithseai@gmail.com.
