The Seafront Pavilion

Bandra West · ₹8.4 Cr · 4 bedrooms · 4,200 sq ft

The residence

Built in 2019 and held by one family since, the house sits behind a gated plot with the sea to the west. The living level opens onto a covered terrace and a 40-foot lap pool; four bedrooms sit on the level above, with the master looking out over water rather than over the neighbours.

Service quarters, a separate entrance and covered parking for three cars are on the lower ground. The house has been lightly used and is being handed over with the original joinery intact.

Where We Work

Three pockets of the city

We stay inside a small radius on purpose. It is the only way to know a street properly.

  • Bandra West

    Sea-facing blocks, older buildings with generous floor plans, and the best of the suburb's food within a few streets. The western edge is quiet; the market road is not.

  • Worli

    Long sea frontage, wide roads, and a lot of glass. Worth watching for older apartments in the sandalwood-lined lanes behind the sea face, which rarely come to market.

  • Powai

    Lake, hills, and the schools. The housing is more consistent than in the southern suburbs, and the Hiranandani-side resale market turns over slowly enough to do proper due diligence.

Notes

Before you view anything

The unglamorous parts of a purchase, written down plainly.

Read the title chain before the floor plan

Most disputes in this city are paperwork, not structural. Ask for the chain of documents, the approved plan, and the society's NOC together, in one sitting. If a seller cannot produce them quickly, that is information.

On societies and their charges

Ask for the last two years of society minutes and the actual ledger, not the summary. Maintenance arrears are a negotiation point for the buyer, and a useful signal about how the building is run.

Why we do not publish market numbers

Registration figures get quoted out of context and turned into advice they cannot support. We would rather tell you what a specific building has actually traded for, what the society charges, and what the same layout is asking three doors away.
