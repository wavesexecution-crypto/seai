01 — The craft

Scissors first, product second.

Every service here starts with a dry assessment and a conversation about how you actually wear your hair. We do not sell add-ons we would not use ourselves.

02 — Services

Cut and colour, in that order.

These are the two things worth photographing. Everything else — gloss, keratin, masks — is in the price list below, and we are happy to do those too.

What a first visit looks like.

  1. 01

    Fifteen minutes of talk

    What you have had done, what you are trying to get away from, and how much maintenance you actually want. No mirror, no product.

  2. 02

    A number before we start

    We agree the price and the time it will take. If the honest answer is "come back in six weeks", that is what we say.

  3. 03

    The work, and a date for next time

    You leave with a written note of what was used, so the next stylist — here or anywhere else — can read it.

04 — The floor

Three rooms, one building.

We are on the third floor, up a narrow stair with no lift. There is no waiting room to speak of — you come up and we pour you tea.

05 — The chair

Three stylists. You pick, or we do.

Everyone here is a working stylist, not a salesperson with a chair. If you would rather not choose, book with any of them.

  • Rehan QureshiCreative director · 14 yrs

    Cuts bobs and blunt shapes. Will talk you out of a fringe you did not really want.

  • Meera IyerSenior colourist · 11 yrs

    Bleach specialist. Owns the strand test, and will recommend waiting six weeks before session two.

  • Kabir ShettyStylist · 6 yrs

    Keratin, smoothing, and the fastest blow-dry on the floor. Also the only one who cuts children well.
