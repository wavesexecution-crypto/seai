Three ways we make it.

Filter, espresso, and whatever came out of the oven that morning. No blends, no flavoured syrups, and no second option when we have run out.

03 — The room

Eighteen seats, no rush.

Front room takes ten and has the street window. Back room takes eight, is quieter, and is where the filter is brewed. Laptops are fine until twelve.

04 — Sourcing

We can name the farm.

One lot at a time, bought through an importer we have used for four years, paid above the going rate. When the harvest is bad we say so rather than blending it away.

05 — The crew

Everyone here has made coffee.

Three on the bar, one in the kitchen. No one here is on the floor only to take your order and clear the table.
