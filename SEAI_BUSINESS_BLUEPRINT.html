1 Brand & Positioning

SEAI is a marketing, advertising and business growth company. Central belief: every business needs exposure — visibility, attention, customers, growth.

◆ What SEAI is

We make businesses visible: branding, ads, social media, websites, lead generation and follow-up systems — delivered as one monthly service.

◆ Problem we solve

Good businesses stay invisible: no strategy, inconsistent posting, ads that "don't work", no follow-up on leads, agencies too expensive.

◆ Brand rule

AI/automation are internal tools only — never the product. We sell marketing, creativity, execution and growth. Clients buy outcomes.

AspectHow it works
Monthly paymentService fee per tier + ad budget paid directly to platforms; third-party costs at cost with approval
Scoping & deliverablesFixed counts per plan (posts, campaigns, pages, reports); signed Scope of Work = single source of truth
Extra workOutside scope = written change request; small = add-on, large = separate project. Never silently absorbed
Ad spend vs feeHard separation; no markup on spend unless a managed-budget model is signed separately
BillingInvoice on the 1st (or anniversary); due in 7 days; GST where applicable
Renewal / cancellationAuto-renews monthly; 15-day written notice; work completes through paid period; assets handed over; no refunds for delivered work

ProfitabilityFee − delivery hours − tools = margin per client; below floor 2 months → scope/pricing review at renewal
